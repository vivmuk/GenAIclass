One key, thirty answers.

Every app here asks the same question the day job asks: can a workflow be governed, repeated and trusted by someone who did not build it? The builds are the evidence.

The flagship sits outside the thirty: KinderQuill, Venice-funded bedtime stories, illustrated and narrated, with a grown-up in the loop. The full private catalogue is at venice.up.railway.app ↗, and how this started is on the About page.
