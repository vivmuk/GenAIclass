Questions

The honest FAQ.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no paid tier, no sponsorship slot and no upsell. If that changes, it will be stated at the top of the email before you are asked for anything.

How is this different from an AI news digest?

A digest tells you what shipped. Katha Weekly argues what it means, and shows the evidence. It also names the strongest counter-argument instead of pretending the thesis is obvious.

How often will you email me?

Once a week, on Sunday. Occasionally a one-line correction if we get something wrong. Nothing else.

How do I unsubscribe?

One click at the bottom of any email. No confirmation maze, no “are you sure” page.

Where does the source material come from?

Public YouTube videos and podcast feeds from eight channels, transcribed and analysed. Every story in an issue links to its source so you can verify the claim yourself.
